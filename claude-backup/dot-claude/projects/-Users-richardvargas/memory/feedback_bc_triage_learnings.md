## Skip Conditions

**Skip when tagged for visibility only.**
If Brittney (or anyone) tags Richard in a comment with "tagging for visibility," he does not respond. No comment needed.
**Why:** Commenting adds noise. He's aware, that's enough.

**Skip when blocked waiting on another person.**
If the last meaningful action belongs to someone else (e.g., Brooke texting the client, Genesis waiting on Nate for access), Richard does not interject.
**Why:** [[feedback-when-not-to-respond]] — silence is intentional, the ball is not in his court.

**Skip when he already commented and is waiting.**
If Richard's last comment asked a question or made a request and no one has responded yet, do not draft a follow-up.
**Why:** Redundant. He already moved the ball.

**Skip on Quarterly Underperformance Review todos (Phases 1-5).**
Richard skipped all of these. They appear to be manager-process todos he does not action directly.

## What Richard Does Not Need Drafted

- Todos he already commented on (he handled it, move on)
- Todos with no description, no comments, and an actively evolving situation (e.g., client cancellation mid-process)
- Todos where the checklist items all belong to someone else

## Weekly Overdue Check (todo 9477524274) — Auto-Bump Rule

If this todo is overdue and has no new comments, automatically move the due date one week out. No need to present it to Richard.

**Why:** It's a recurring weekly task. If it slips, just extend it — no comment needed unless someone has flagged something in the thread.

---

## Delegation Before Responding

When a todo requires Creative to take action (edit footage, design something, etc.), Richard creates a delegation todo and links it to the comment before responding. Do not draft a standalone comment in these cases — flag it as "needs delegation todo first" so Richard can handle it manually.

**Why:** He wants the delegation and the comment to be connected. Responding without the delegation todo in place is incomplete.

---

## "Manual" With No Notes = Draft Was Perfect

When Richard says "manual" with no additional notes or edits, it means the draft was exactly right — he just wants to post it himself. No changes needed. Do not ask follow-up questions.

When Richard says "manual" AND provides notes or edits inline, that means he wants to modify the draft before posting.

---

## Time of Day — After Hours

Richard does not post Basecamp comments late at night, even when the draft is approved. If it's evening (after ~6-7pm ET), note that the draft is ready and let him post it the next business day.

**Why:** He's mindful about not working after hours and doesn't want to send communications at unusual times.
