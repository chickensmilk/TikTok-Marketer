# Inbound Email Campaign — Opening To-Dos SOP

## Purpose
Quarterly process for opening and configuring email campaign to-dos in Basecamp, working backwards from the client's Target Send Date.

## Prerequisites
- Approved email topics confirmed by client/AM for the quarter
- Strategy doc or to-do comment created with campaign details
- Target send dates known

## To-Do Naming Convention
`[PHASE] – [Month] – Email Name`
Example: `DESIGN – April – Spring Clean Your Tech`

## Project Timeline Algorithm (work backwards from Target Send Date)

| Milestone | Timing Rule |
|-----------|-------------|
| TARGET SEND DATE | Client's confirmed send date/range |
| Client Approval Needed by | 2–3 business days before start of send range |
| Client Test HDD | 7–10 business days before Client Approval date |
| Build HDD | At least 1 business day before Client Test HDD |
| Design HDD | At least 3 business days before Client Test HDD |
| Content HDD | At least 1 week before Design HDD |

**Key rule:** Protect the Client Test HDD above all other milestones when timelines are tight.

## Team Assignments per Phase
- **[WRITE]** → Content Team
- **[DESIGN], [BUILD], [TEST], [SCHEDULE/SEND]** → Creative Team (designer assigned by ACM)
- **[CLIENT TEST], [REVIEW]** → Account Manager

## Who Sets the Dates
The **Assistant Creative Manager (Brittney)** is responsible for setting all milestone due dates using the algorithm above.

## Notes
- Most clients: 1 email send per month = 1 to-do per campaign
- If send date is a range, use the start of the range to calculate Client Approval date
- Always verify the correct option number matches what was approved
