# Vendor Management SOP — Standard Multimedia (Photo & Video)

## Purpose
Process for sourcing and managing outside vendors when someone outside the team must shoot for clients.

## Before Starting
Have ready: clear intention, scope of work, client city (only — do not share address/name in initial outreach).

## I. Vendor Selection
1. Search 3–5 vendors via Google or Instagram — prioritize professional/commercial work in client's local area
2. Evaluate portfolio: commercial experience, footage quality without edit, niche match
3. Send initial outreach email with scope details + request for estimate (full/half-day or hourly)
   - ⚠️ Do NOT share budget or client name/address in initial outreach
   - Log all contacts in Vendor Contacts spreadsheet
4. Monitor communications (may take several days); advance to phone only when moving forward
   - English fluency is required on-site — disqualifier if not met
5. Add estimates to Basecamp to-do for leadership review/approval (Shay/Rich)
6. After approval: contact vendor for formal quote, lock in date (phone call to build rapport)

## II. Onboarding & Contracting
- Share vendor agreement/contract
- Create calendar invite (vendor, client, AM, Multimedia Specialist)
- Prepare call sheet and shot list
- Create Google Drive folders for delivery
- Schedule project kickoff with vendor to review call sheet + scope + post-shoot instructions
- Final touchbase to confirm vendor has reviewed all materials

## III. Delivery
- Post-shoot follow-up via AM with questions: how did it go, anything not captured, client feedback?
- Vendor uploads deliverables to pre-made Google Drive links
- Multimedia Specialist QAs deliverables
- Coordinate vendor payment via Accounting
